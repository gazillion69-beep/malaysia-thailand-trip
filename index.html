Use Phuket Airport (HKT), not Krabi. Pullman Panwa → HKT is roughly 42–43 km and commonly around 45–60 minutes by car depending on traffic.

Motorcycle / scooter

Indian licence was issued in Maharashtra. Before riding in Thailand, verify current IDP, motorcycle entitlement, rental and insurance requirements from official sources.
